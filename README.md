# ELPA21 Listening Practice · Unit 1

Interactive listening practice for English learners (grades 9–12), modeled on the ELPA21 listening task types.

**Live site:** https://radan55.github.io/elpa21practice01/

## What's inside
- **Lesson 1 · Listen and Match the Word** — 4 items (Places, Sky, Ways to go, Animals). Students hear a direction and tap the matching picture.
- **Lesson 2 · Listen and Match the Sentence** — 4 items (Restaurant, In the car, Home and town, Trips). Students hear a sentence and tap the matching scene.
- **Lesson 4 · Listen to a Conversation** — questions 1–10 across two conversations between students (a math assignment; a school garden). Two device voices, a highlighted transcript, multiple-choice questions read aloud.
- **Lesson 3 · Listen for Information** — questions 1–10 across 4 pages (Lab tools, Liquids, Soil layers, Alaska and Hawaii). Students hear a short passage, then follow each direction by tapping a card and tapping where it goes.

## Supports
Slow voice, Show text, Show words, instant feedback with retry, first-try score per item.

## Notes
- Audio uses the device's built-in text-to-speech voice (no files to load). Sound varies by device.
- All passages, sentences and drawings are original EL Publishing practice material.
- Single file: `index.html`. No account, nothing is saved or collected.
